neymar

Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior (born February 5, 1992) is a Brazilian professional footballer widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time. Known for his mesmerizing dribbling, technical ability, and playmaking, he currently captains his boyhood club, Santos, and stars for the Brazil national team
